# Basic Facts

- It locates in "The Hearth of Europe" (cental Europe)
- It has about 10.9 milion inhabitants
- covers area of nearly 79 thousand sq km​
- **Capital city**: Prague
- **Currency:** Czech Crown
- It has 4 neighbours (Poland, Germany, Slovakia and Austria )
- Consist of 3 main parts
	- Bohemia
	- Moravia
	- Silesia
- 14 main regions, including the capital city Prague

# Geography

- Mountain ranges: main are **Krkonoše** highest mountain **Sněžka**, 1603 m), **Šumava**, **Jeseníky** and **Beskydy**.
- Lowlands *(Polabí, Podyjí, Hornomoravský and Dolnomoravský úval)*
- Highlands *(Českomoravská vrchovina, Středočeská pahorkatina,..)*
- Rivers:  **Vltava** (goes through Prague, 430 km), **Elbe (Labe)** and **Morava**.
- 2 glacial lakes (Černé and Čertovo in the Šumava Mountains)
- many dams (Orlík, Lipno, Slapy on the Vltava, Švihov on the Želivka, Vranov on the Dyje,..)

#### Climate

- **temperate**
- 4 seasons of the year

#### Ecology

- the Czech Republic has above-average greenhouse gas emissions within the European Union​
- lack of ground water
- in springs and summers storms, gales, torrential rains and floods​

# Politics

- parliamentary republic​
- the **President** is the head of state and the **Prime Minister** is the head of government​
- government consists of the **Chamber of Deputies** (200 members) and the **Senate** (81 members)​
- political system of the Czech Republic is a Multi-Party System
